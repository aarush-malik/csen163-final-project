Quick Tutorial

  1. Go to AI Search and type a title, author, or topic.
  2. Use the filters for category, availability, and sorting.
  3. Open a result to view exact floor, aisle, and shelf directions.
  4. Reserve available books or join the waitlist for checked-out titles.
  5. Visit Sustainability to track environmental impact from borrowing.

FAQ

  • What is SCAIL? SCAIL stands for Santa Clara AI Library, a prototype smart library interface inspired by SCU workflows.
  • Do I need an account? No account is required to browse and search the catalog in this demo.
  • Can I borrow digital books? Titles marked “Digital Available” can be accessed digitally when available.
  • How accurate are the location directions? The app provides aisle/shelf guidance from our catalog metadata and floor map.
