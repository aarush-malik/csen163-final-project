SCAIL Help Center

Everything you need to get started with Santa Clara AI Library — from quick tutorial steps to FAQs and support contacts.

Quick Tutorial

  1. Go to AI Search and type a title, author, or topic.
  2. Use filters for category, availability, and sorting.
  3. Open a result to view exact floor, aisle, and shelf directions.
  4. Reserve available books or join the waitlist for checked-out titles.
  5. Visit Sustainability to track your environmental impact.

FAQ

  • What is SCAIL? It stands for Santa Clara AI Library, inspired by SCU library workflows.
  • Do I need an account? Not for browsing/searching this demo catalog.
  • Are digital books supported? Yes, for titles marked “Digital Available.”
  • How accurate is shelf guidance? Directions come from the catalog metadata + floor map.
